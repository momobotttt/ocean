取代《教學安排》第十章原本嘅「分工」寫法　·　授課語言 全英文（下面英文句子照讀）
呢份嘢係一個修正。原本《教學安排》第十章寫到好似「技術＝唔關你事，交返俾 Dr Lee」。嗰個寫法唔啱，而且會令你交走自己最好嘅內容。 四堂入面最強嘅幾個教學時刻 —— proxy variable、threshold、data leakage、unexplained gap —— 全部都係技術連繫位。 交咗出去，你就淨返講故事。呢份文件重新劃條線。

一、唔係兩邊，係三層

層邊個講內容
一　個決定你，全部呢個模型係為咗答邊條 HR 問題？答完之後邊個要做嘢？做錯嘅成本係幾多？邊個有權睇？
二　個連繫
← 重點喺呢層		你，一定要講到個模型實際做緊乜、佢個輸出係咩形狀、佢點解會錯、佢答唔到啲咩。概念層，唔使數學，唔使程式
三　個機制Dr Lee演算法點運作、點訓練、點調參、點驗證、統計推導

第二層先係你嘅價值所在，而且係最少人教得好嗰層。Dr Lee 教得到第三層，教科書寫咗第一層 —— 但係由第三層行返去第一層嗰條路，邊個教？因為要行呢條路，你要同時知道HR 會議入面實際發生咩事，同埋個模型實際交咗啲乜出嚟。全個課程得你一個人兩樣都知。

條線唔係劃喺「技術 vs 唔技術」，係劃喺「深度」。
「呢個模型會俾一個 0 到 1 之間嘅分數，你要自己揀喺邊度切」→ 你講　　「佢用 gradient boosting，learning rate 0.1」→ Dr Lee 講

三、四堂各自嘅技術連繫落點

星期日 1　人力規劃　連繫：份計劃本身就係一個模型

幾時：兩段。活動之前 2 分鐘（立框架）＋ 匯報時 3 分鐘（收成果）。合共 5 分鐘。

活動之前（第 78 分鐘）

"What you are about to build is a model. The formulas are fixed — that is the structure. The cells you can change are assumptions. Every model in this course has those same two parts. And when a plan fails, it is almost never the structure. It is an assumption nobody wrote down."

三組匯報時（第 103–116 分鐘）—— 呢度先係 payoff

"Every group used the same spreadsheet. Look at the spread in your answers. Same structure, different assumptions, completely different plans. That spread has a name — model risk — and it is why the last thing I asked each of you for was the assumption that breaks everything."

學生會問：「咁點樣先估到個啱嘅流失率？」→ 你答："From your own history, and from the market. How to do that properly is Dr Lee's." 然後留一句："But notice: nobody in this room asked for a range. You all took a single number."

星期日 2　招聘　連繫：刪走個欄唔會刪走個資訊

幾時：公平性審查（第 78–100 分鐘）中間，當有組講「咁刪走嗰個特徵咪得囉」嗰刻。4 分鐘。呢個係全堂最好嘅四分鐘。

"Someone always says: just delete the field. Here is why that does not work. A model does not need the field — it needs anything that correlates with it. University. Postcode. A gap in the CV. The information is still sitting in your data. It has just changed its name. So deleting the column does not remove the bias. It removes your ability to audit it."

再加一句拉返 HR：

"Which means the fix is not technical. Somebody has to decide what the process is allowed to look at, write it down, and then check. That is not a data scientist's job."

順手做埋：impact ratio 同四分之五法則係純算術，你完全擁有 —— 唔好講到好似要交出去。

星期日 3　薪酬　連繫：因果推論，同「無法解釋」到底解釋緊咩

幾時：講授 C（第 55–72 分鐘）入面 2 分鐘講 residual；300 萬個案（第 77–103 分鐘）入面 3 分鐘講 DiD。合共 5 分鐘。

講 unexplained gap 嗰陣

"When you hear 'unexplained pay gap', understand what that number is. It is a residual — what is left over after you account for level, tenure and performance. It is not proof of discrimination. It is also not proof of innocence. It is the part your model cannot account for, and that is exactly why it is worth looking at."

300 萬個案入面

"Difference-in-differences is not complicated. You need a group that got the money, a group that did not, and you need to know what both were doing beforehand. Then you compare the change, not the level. Everything genuinely hard about it is choosing those two groups — and that is an HR decision, not a statistical one."
呢一句就係你成堂嘅論點：技術上最難嗰部分（因果推論），佢個難處係喺 HR 判斷嗰邊，唔係喺數學嗰邊。所以呢一堂唔應該交俾 Dr Lee 講。

五、交棒點做先啱：先答 HR 嗰半，再交機器嗰半

原本嗰三句係「點樣避開」。真正做得好嘅交棒係兩段式：我答到你嘅 X，Y 就要問 Dr Lee。咁樣你唔係退，你係劃界。

學生問你答
"What algorithm should we use?""For this decision the algorithm matters far less than where you set the threshold — and that one is ours. Which algorithm, and why, is Dr Lee's, and he will take it further than I can."
"How accurate is it?""Accuracy is the wrong question here, and I can show you why in thirty seconds. [講 class imbalance] The right question is what each kind of error costs you. How to measure it properly — Dr Lee."
"How does it weight that variable?""I can't tell you the weighting. I can tell you that weighting is not the same as cause, and I can tell you exactly what happens when a manager receives that number and has to act on it — which is the part that goes wrong."
"Can we just use AI for this?""You can. Then you still have to choose the threshold, decide who sees the list, and answer a data access request. None of those go away. [然後入私隱條例]"
真係唔識嗰啲"I don't know. My instinct is X, but don't quote me — I'll check and come back to you next Sunday."
下星期真係要返返嚟講。四堂之間隔一個星期，呢個係你最抵用嘅資產：查完，第二堂開場三十秒答返上星期條問題。全班會記住呢件事，多過記住答案本身。

六、同 Dr Lee 講：唔係「避開重疊」，係「我鋪路，你落深」

原本嗰個協調問題係「有冇嘢我唔應該講」。改做：明確話俾佢知你會用非技術語言 set 起邊幾個概念，佢就可以直接接住落去，唔使由頭嚟過。呢個對佢係著數，唔係麻煩。

Email 大意（英文）：
"Rather than avoid the technical material, I'd like to set it up for you in plain language and stop there. Across my four Sundays I'll introduce, conceptually and without any maths: model structure versus assumptions; validity of selection methods; proxy variables; the confusion matrix priced in dollars; the decision threshold as a management choice; class imbalance; data leakage; and the difference between a predictive feature and a cause. I won't touch algorithms, training, tuning or validation — those are yours, and students will arrive at them already knowing why they matter. Tell me if you'd rather I left any of these alone, or if you'd like me to frame one of them differently so it lands where you need it."

唯一要動手嘅一件事（做咗，第四堂會完全唔同）：
喺 Excel 開一張四十行嘅表，加一欄 0 至 1 嘅風險分數，再加一欄「實際有冇走」。然後自己拉個 threshold：0.5、0.3、0.7。 每次數吓：捉錯咗幾多個（白花錢）、走漏咗幾多個（走咗但冇預到）。 你親手見過嗰兩個數字係點樣反方向郁，你堂上就講得出，而且唔會虛。呢個就係全份指南入面最重要嗰一句嘅實物版。
